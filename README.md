## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Mechatronics Engineering graduate from Tecnológico de Monterrey** with a strong interest in **robotics, artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, computer vision and industrial automation**.

I build and integrate engineering systems spanning **mechanical design, embedded hardware, sensors, motor control, machine perception and software**. My portfolio includes autonomous mobile robots, vision-guided robotics and **12 completed programming projects from Harvard's CS50's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python**.

- **Engineering:** Mechatronics, CAD, electronics and robotics integration.
- **AI:** Machine learning, deep learning, computer vision and NLP.
- **Systems:** NVIDIA Jetson, Intel RealSense, VESC motor control and industrial automation.
- **Approach:** Prototype → integrate → test → measure → improve.
